## Assets generated by this project

- **Launcher icon.** Generated by `tools/GenerateIcons.java` from a geometric
  definition written for this project. It is not derived from any icon set, so no
  attribution is required. The glyph is drawn as three capsules (a curved grip and
  two pads) and rendered with Java2D for the bitmaps and as a vector drawable for
  the adaptive and monochrome layers.
- **Sample exchange archive.** `design/fixtures/SilverPhone_联系人_测试包.zip` is
  generated by `tools/GenerateSampleArchive.java`. Every photo in it is drawn from
  shapes — a flat background, a circle and a rounded rectangle. It contains no
  photograph of any person and no real personal data. The phone numbers in it are
  placeholders and must not be dialled.

## Icons in the UI

The in-app glyphs come from `androidx.compose.material:material-icons-core`, which is
part of AndroidX and covered by the Apache-2.0 entry above.

## Not included

There is no bundled third-party font, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no
advertising SDK, no network library and no native `.so` beyond those pulled in
transitively by the components above. There is no cryptography library: the exchange
archive is deliberately unencrypted, and the only cryptographic primitive used is
SHA-256 from the JDK's own `java.security.MessageDigest`, for integrity checking
rather than secrecy.
